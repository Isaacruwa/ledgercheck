Frequently asked

Is this free to use on my site?

Yes. No charge, no account, no permission needed — just copy the code.

Will it slow down my site?

It loads as a small iframe with no tracking scripts or heavy dependencies, so the impact on load time is minimal.

Does it collect any data from my visitors?

No. Numbers entered are processed entirely in the visitor's browser inside the embedded frame and are never sent to a server — the same privacy model as the main EOBCheck tool.
